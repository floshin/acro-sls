Fleurs de la Sarre

Ein Generationen übergreifendes Ensemble von Akrobaten in und um Saarlouis. Die Technik der Sportakrobatik vereint mit der kreativen Freiheit des zeitgenössischen Zirkus.

Show

  • Among the Waves of the Moon

    Our first show will accompany two girls, acrobatic flowers in growth, trying to cross the ocean of their childhood, getting carried away by the nymphs. A story told through acrobatic movement. Minimal stage, costume and light design.

    With music by Clann.
